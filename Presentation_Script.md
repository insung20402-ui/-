## Slide 3 — Song Introduction

> So, what is this song about? This song is about exercise. It uses a strong warning message to make people want to move. The core message is: "If you do not move, tomorrow will be worse." Through this idea, the song shows why exercise is so important. Exercise helps both our body and our mind — it improves fitness, mental health, and our daily life. We hope this song makes you think about the importance of exercise, and gives you motivation to move through a strong, honest warning.

## Slide 4 — Meaning and Examples of Strong Warning Messages

> Now let's look more closely at what we mean by a "strong warning message." Our key phrase is: "If you do not move, tomorrow will be worse." This is a message designed to lead people to action. We also looked at what happens when people don't exercise — low fitness, low energy, and poor health. By placing "No Exercise" next to "Exercise," we create a clear contrast that motivates people to choose the healthier path. A strong warning message works because it shows a real problem. Saying "Move now, or your future will be worse" makes the need for exercise feel urgent and personal.

---

## Slide 5 — Negative Effects

> Let's talk about the negative effects of not exercising.
>
> First, **low fitness and disease risk**: lack of exercise weakens our muscles and heart health, and increases the risk of obesity, diabetes, and heart disease.
>
> Second, **worse mental health**: being inactive can increase stress, anxiety, and sadness, and it can reduce our sleep quality.
>
> Third, **lower quality of life**: without exercise, people may feel tired, lose focus, and enjoy daily life less.
>
> In short, a lack of exercise has serious effects on both our health and our daily life — and that's exactly the warning our song is sending.
