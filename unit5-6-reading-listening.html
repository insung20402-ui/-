본문 읽기

My Hometown, Chuncheon

Hi, everyone! I'm Sujin and I want to tell you about my hometown, Chuncheon. It's in Gangwon Province, and it's a beautiful city with mountains and lakes.

Chuncheon is famous for dakgalbi. Dakgalbi is a spicy chicken dish, and it's one of the most popular foods to try in Chuncheon. There are many restaurants to visit on Dakgalbi Street.

Have you ever been to Nami Island? It's a beautiful place to see, especially in autumn. The trees along the road are so beautiful that many visitors take pictures there.

Chuncheon also has a famous puppet festival every summer. I have been to the festival three times. It was wonderful! If you haven't visited Chuncheon yet, I recommend it. You will have a great time!

분홍 표시: to부정사(형용사적 용법) / 현재완료 표현이 본문에 쓰였어요.

본문 읽기

My First Skateboarding Lesson

Last Saturday, my friend Tom gave me a skateboarding lesson. I was so nervous because I had never tried it before. Tom said, "Don't worry. I'll make you feel comfortable."

First, Tom let me stand on the board. He made me hold his hands for balance. I heard the wheels rolling under my feet, and I felt the board moving slowly.

Then, I tried to push and ride by myself. I fell down many times, but Tom was very patient. He let me take breaks and encouraged me to try again.

Finally, I could ride the skateboard without falling. I saw Tom smiling at me, and I felt so proud. It was the best day! I learned that trying new things is not easy, but it's always worth it.

분홍 표시: 지각동사(see, hear, feel) + 목적어 + V/V-ing · 사역동사(make, let) + 목적어 + 동사원형이 본문에 쓰였어요.
